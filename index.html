About

About me

I’m Zac, a photographer, videographer, editor and musician based on the Sunshine Coast, Queensland.

My work grows out of live music, skate culture and creative collaborations. I enjoy capturing people doing what they love and shaping those moments into photographs, videos and artwork.

Playing in No Splits gives me a perspective from both sides of the camera. I bring that understanding to working with artists, businesses and other creatives, from the first conversation to the finished piece.
